## 👋 About me

I'm a **Data Scientist** and **ML Engineer** from Málaga, Spain, with a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from the University of Málaga. I like tackling complex problems and turning them into practical solutions.

- 💼 Data Scientist at **Plain Concepts**, building AI solutions with **LLMs** and **Machine Learning** for clients
- 🔭 Interested in **Machine Learning**, **Data Science**, **Web Development** and **Game Development**
- 🎮 I make small games and game-jam entries, and tinker with Raspberry Pi / ESP32 hardware
- 🐧 Linux enthusiast, with a lot of time spent on dotfiles
- 📫 Best way to reach me: the [contact form on my portfolio](https://deinigu.github.io/portfolio/contact) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dlreduello/)
